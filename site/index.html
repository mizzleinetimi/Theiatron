What it does

It sees. It measures. It decides.

The same loop a traffic officer runs with their eyes — at machine speed, all day, on hardware that costs less than a phone.

It sees

An on-device detector finds and tracks every vehicle on every approach, ten times a second — cars, buses, bikes, trucks.

It measures

Waiting is the enemy. Pixels become how many are stuck on each road, and how long they've been stuck.

It decides

Green goes to the pressure — behind hard safety interlocks: never two greens, and a fairness cap so no road is forgotten.

Why I'm knocking on your door

I need 30 minutes at your window

The footage above is free-licensed stock from another country. For the challenge finale, Theiatron should run on our own traffic — and that takes one thing I can't get from the ground: an upper window, balcony, or rooftop overlooking an intersection.

The ask — simple and supervised

  • 30–45 minutes, daylight. A phone on a small tripod, pointed at the road junction. That's the whole setup.
  • Escorted the entire time. I don't need to wander — walk me to the spot and stay with me.
  • Nothing of yours is filmed. The camera looks down at the public road — never into any building or your premises.
  • Privacy by design. The AI counts vehicles only. It identifies no one, and plates or faces are blurred on request.

What you get

  • Named credit — your building or business listed as the filming location in the project.
  • First look — your intersection analyzed, with its wait-time savings quantified.
  • A stake in it — a hand in a local innovation headed to a national challenge finale.
