Reconstruction accuracy

This is a planar reconstruction from detected bottom-centre anchors, not a surveyed digital twin. Road edges are estimated from image appearance and traffic support, or manually traced. Camera perspective is estimated from static scene directions when supported, assuming a pinhole camera, orthogonal horizontal directions, upright structures and flat ground. Metre scale is inherited from an assumed reference span, not measured. Image borders and occlusions still limit the road footprint; not every outline edge is a kerb. Vehicle sizes and colours are illustrative; retained detections choose the model class when available. Occlusions, missed vehicles and ID switches remain visible in the evidence.

Gaps over 0.61 s and flagged discontinuities are not bridged. Offline trajectory filtering is optional and never changes saved observations. No traffic savings are computed here.
